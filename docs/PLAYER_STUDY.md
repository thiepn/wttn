# Five-player acceptance study — ready to run, not yet performed

Recruit five people who have not played this game. Observe each independently for ten minutes from a clean save, then five minutes after an ordinary offline interval. Use at least two phones if available. Do not teach the controls or explain resets before the participant attempts the tasks.

Ask: “Develop the settlement as you think useful.” At minutes 2 and 8 ask “What would you do next, and why?” Before the first Translation ask “What will you gain, lose and keep?” After return ask “What happened while you were away?” Ask participants to point to the visual change caused by one purchase.

Record next action found without help, first reset explained correctly, purchase-to-world change identified, time to first useful decision, any blocked controls, and return recap understood. The acceptance threshold is at least four of five for each of the first three outcomes.

Separately ask enjoyment (1–7), desire to return tomorrow (1–7), least satisfying moment and most satisfying moment. Do not combine these subjective results with task-completion scores. Do not infer fun from automated success.
